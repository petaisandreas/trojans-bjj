About Our Academy

TROJANS BJJ is a Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu academy in Larnaca, Cyprus, led by certified coaches dedicated to teaching authentic, effective BJJ in a supportive, disciplined environment. We offer classes for adults of all levels, as well as a dedicated kids program starting from age 3, focused on building confidence, discipline and self-defense skills through the art of Jiu-Jitsu.

Adults — All Levels

From complete beginners to competitors, our adult classes cover fundamentals, technique, drilling and live rolling.

Kids Program (3yo+)

A fun, structured program that builds coordination, confidence and discipline for young athletes.

Private Lessons

One-on-one coaching with Eleftheria or Venizelos, tailored to your goals and pace.
