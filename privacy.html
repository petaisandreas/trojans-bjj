Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 August 2026

This policy explains what personal information TROJANS BJJ collects through this website, why we collect it, and what rights you have over it. It applies only to this website.

Who we are

TROJANS BJJ is a Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu academy based in Larnaca, Cyprus. For anything relating to your personal data, contact us on WhatsApp at +357 97 605817.

What we collect

We only collect what you type into the booking form yourself. Nothing is gathered in the background, and this site sets no cookies of its own.

  • Your name and phone number
  • The session type you selected
  • Your chosen payment method
  • Your preferred date and time, if you give them
  • Any optional message you write

How your booking reaches us

This website has no database and no server that stores your details. When you submit the booking form, your browser opens WhatsApp with those details written into a message addressed to us. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp, and you can edit or discard the message first. Once sent, the message sits in our WhatsApp chat history and is handled by WhatsApp under its own privacy policy.

Why we use it

Solely to respond to your booking request — confirming availability, arranging your class and answering your questions. We do not use your details for marketing, and we do not sell or share them with anyone for their own purposes.

How long we keep it

Your booking message stays in our WhatsApp chat history until we delete it. You can ask us to delete it at any time and we will do so.

Other services this site uses

A few third-party services are involved in delivering this page. Each receives your IP address as a normal part of serving content, and each has its own privacy policy:

  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website
  • Google Fonts — supplies the typefaces used here
  • Google Maps — provides the embedded map in the contact section
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — carries your booking message to us

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to show you the personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or restrict how we use it. You can also withdraw your consent at any time. Message us on the number above and we will action it. If you believe we have mishandled your data, you may complain to the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection in Cyprus.

Children

Our kids classes are booked by a parent or guardian. If you are booking for a child, please submit the form yourself rather than having the child do it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will appear on this page with a new date at the top.
